Behind the stall

Everything here was printed at home.

Fidget Forge started as a Year 9 Design & Technology project and turned into an actual table at an actual market. Every model is sliced, printed, cleaned up and tested before it makes it onto the stand.

The designs are aimed at kids aged 6 to 14 first — they're the ones who use fidget toys most — but plenty of the range gets bought by high schoolers, parents who fidget through meetings, and people looking for a small gift that isn't another keyring.

Printing at home means small batches, odd colours, and the occasional experiment that only exists once. If you see something you like, it might not be there next month.
